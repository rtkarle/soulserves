Our Story

More Than a Platform.
A Movement.

SoulServe was born from a simple belief — that technology, compassion and community can together solve problems that have existed for generations.

Our Origin

Born from a Problem
We Couldn't Ignore.

Every day in India, millions of kilograms of cooked food are thrown away — while millions of people go to sleep hungry. Mountains of clothing are discarded — while families lack basic dignity.

SoulServe started with one question: "What if we could connect the surplus with the shortage?"

The answer became a technology platform powered by AI, driven by compassion — connecting donors, volunteers, beneficiaries and rural artisans in one seamless ecosystem.

💡

The Problem We Solve

40% of food in India is wasted — enough to feed 200 million people

Millions of wearable clothes discarded annually while families lack basics

Rural artisans with no marketplace access, losing generational skills

Volunteers willing to help but no coordinated platform to work through

Our Journey

How SoulServe Evolved

2024

Idea Born

Started with a single food rescue during a college event. 40 kg of food, 60 families fed.

Early 2025

Platform Built

PHP + MySQL + Python AI stack developed. Donation matching, volunteer recommendation live.

Mid 2025

Marketplace Added

Rural artisans onboarded. SoulServe Marketplace launched with 20+ sellers across Maharashtra.

Late 2025

AI Upgraded

4 ML models trained with 76–92% accuracy. Flask AI API serving real-time recommendations.

2026

Deployed Live

Live on Render + Railway + Cloudinary. Email notifications, delivery proof photos, admin panel.
